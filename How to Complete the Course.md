# 📢 2400 – Python Project Foundation: How to Complete the Course

Welcome to **2400 – Python Project Foundation**!

Please follow the course in order, starting from **Lesson 1 through Lesson 10**. Each lesson is designed to build on the concepts from the previous lesson, so we strongly recommend that you do not skip ahead.

## How to Complete Each Lesson

For every lesson, please follow these steps:

1. **Read the Class Notes**

   * Open the PDF file under **ClassNotes**.
   * Review the concepts, examples, and exercises included in the notes.

2. **Watch the Lesson Recording**

   * Open the video under **Lesson Recordings**.
   * The instructor will walk through the class notes, explain important Python concepts, demonstrate examples, and guide you through the exercises.

3. **Complete the Exercises**

   * Work through the exercises for the lesson yourself.
   * Try to solve each problem before looking at the provided solutions.

4. **Complete the Quiz**

   * Take the quiz for each lesson to check your understanding of the concepts.

5. **Complete the Homework**

   * Finish the homework assignments for each lesson.
   * The exercises and homework are an important part of developing your Python programming skills.

## Exercise and Homework Solutions

Exercise solutions, homework solutions, and solution videos are provided in each lesson.

Please use these materials as **references after you have attempted the problems yourself**. You will learn much more by trying to solve the problems first and then comparing your work with the provided solutions.

You are encouraged to submit your **exercise solutions and homework solutions**, but submission is **not required**.

## Lesson 10 – Final Exam

Lesson 10 includes the **Final Exam**, and **every student is required to complete it**.

If you receive a final exam score of **80% or higher**, you have demonstrated that you have mastered the basic foundations of Python programming and are ready to continue to the **Data Science Bootcamp**.

The most important goal is not simply to finish the lessons, but to make sure you understand the concepts and can write Python programs independently.
